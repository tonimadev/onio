Onio is a shopping simulator for people who love the thrill of buying but want to keep their savings.

Browse a full store, add items to your cart and go through checkout just like in a real shop. A little while later, every order is automatically cancelled and nothing is ever charged.

See how much money you didn't spend in your Savings tab.

Important: Onio is a simulation. No real purchase is made, no payment is processed, and card details are never stored or sent anywhere.
